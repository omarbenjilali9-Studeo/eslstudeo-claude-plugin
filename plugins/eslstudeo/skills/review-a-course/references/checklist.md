# Review checklist

## Correct
- Every answer key is right. In `mc`, exactly one option is right. In `multi`, every right option is
  in `answers` and no wrong one is.
- Every acceptable answer to a gap is listed with a pipe (`[[colour|color]]`), including contracted
  forms and common equivalent wordings.
- No spelling or grammar mistakes in instructions, content, questions or options, apart from the
  deliberate mistakes in error-correction items.
- Every page that refers to a picture, a recording or a document has it.
- Every `truefalse` statement is clearly true or clearly false from the text.

## Measures what it claims to measure
- The right `mc` option is not always in the same position, not the longest, and not the only option
  that fits the question grammatically.
- Every wrong option is plausible; none is absurd.
- Comprehension options paraphrase the text instead of copying its words.
- No matching pair shares a word, or a form of the same word, that gives it away.
- Each gap has enough context to decide the answer.
- Practice uses a new situation, not the text the learners have just read.

## Page design (the house style)
- One job per page.
- A heading (the stage) and a title on every page.
- Instructions as short numbered steps, simpler than the content.
- Every exercise title reads as a task.
- Polls ask for opinions only.

## Order of learning
- Within each unit, recognition comes before production.
- Difficult forms (spellings, formulas, grammar patterns) are practised with gaps or error
  correction, not only with choices.
- Where the aim is to write or to speak, the unit contains an open question, a role-play or a
  discussion.
- The unit ends with a check or a summary.

## Level
- Vocabulary and sentence length suit the learners, and the instructions are easier than the
  content.
- A unit introduces no more new material than one lesson can hold.

## Settings
- A test uses exam mode (one sitting), results at the end or none, and a time limit if it is timed.
- A diagnostic is untagged and silent (`feedback` "none").
- The course itself carries no term dates; dates belong to classes.
- If a certificate is on, its conditions suit the course.

## Support for teachers
- Pages whose design a teacher might question carry `teacherNotes` (the aim, the traps).
- Every open question has marking guidance: what it is marked out of, criteria, bands and a model
  answer.
